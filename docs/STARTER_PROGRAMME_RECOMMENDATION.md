# NIU Starter Programme Recommendation

## Proposed framework

NIU’s first proposed offering is the **Certificate in Digital Skills, Entrepreneurship, and Remote Work**. This is an optional form prefill for the protected Institutional Builder, not a pre-created or published programme. An authorised NIU administrator must select an existing department, review the framework, save it as a draft, build its courses and materials, and pass the existing programme-package readiness controls before publication.

The proposed framework begins at a beginner level and covers inclusive digital communication and collaboration, privacy and information evaluation, opportunity recognition, and responsible project planning. It does **not** award a degree, licence, professional certification, employment guarantee, accreditation, partnership status, or recognition by any external body.

## Design rationale

UNESCO identifies digital competencies as important for participation in learning and employment and highlights inclusion, accessibility, gender equality, and the digital divide as essential design considerations. [1] The EntreComp framework describes entrepreneurial learning as the ability to act on opportunities and ideas to create financial, cultural, or social value; it organizes that learning under Ideas & Opportunities, Resources, and Into Action. [2]

NIU uses these sources only as high-level design references. The NIU programme remains independent and must be reviewed by its authorised academic team for local laws, accessibility needs, learner protection, assessment validity, and copyright before any public release.
